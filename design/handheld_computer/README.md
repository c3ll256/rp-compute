## Horizontal FPC reach review
The native tail length13.7±0.5mm is an authorized estimate, not a measured or toleranced supplier length. The screen and locator remain unchanged. Native glass exit is worldY=-0.5630557675,Z=27.9236985818mm. Board finished top is18.3mm. Even treating the entire2.2mm maximum closed connector height as the possible cable plane gives at mostZ20.5mm, requiring at least7.423699mm vertical drop.
Under a conservative interpretation keeping curvature inside Sharp’s recommended0.8..6.0mm unfolded region, an optimistic bound is0.8*sin13deg+5.2=5.379961mm drop; this even ignores finite bend radius. Thus the retained stack lacks at least2.043738mm of height allowance. The estimate does not prove bending outside the recommended region impossible, but that route has no supplier-backed qualification. Length allowance alone does not establish mating.
The historical inverted180deg fold was designed for a tilted reference connector and is not the current horizontal PCB mating path. Its contact direction also cannot be carried over unchanged into the top-mounted bottom-contact socket. No physical tail or insertion stop has been tested. Keep the horizontal connector direction. A small raised horizontal FPC daughterboard would relax the single-rigid-board requirement; keeping one board needs GPIO stack/connector and front-module deck reconciliation. Neither user-owned requirement is changed here.

## Making and assembly
Visual study only: do not order/print as a working enclosure. Walls nominal2.4mm, detachable bottom with a visual seam; no retention features or fasteners yet. Parts may sit with intentional assembly clearances and are not fixed together. Material colours: ivory shell/lid, orange display housing/button, charcoal bezel. No physical assembly, support strategy, print calibration or fit test has been performed. Powered use and battery installation are not authorized by this CAD deliverable.

The rear-loading locator pocket is supplemented by the retained rear frame as described above. Proposed later assembly is screen into removed pod from rear, fit low-stress support/retainer, connect tail with full latch access, then attach pod. Do not suspend LCD by FPC, stick adhesive on polarizer or bend at glass edge. Enlarged interior deck opening and thin front regions require strength checks. Failed printed-screw-kit candidates committed no geometry; retain their failure record rather than substituting hand-modeled screw joints.
