# connector_fit_references

Drawing-based nominal fit reference for the selected Samtec connector. This is deliberately modeled from the manufacturer's dimensions because a suitable vendor STEP could not be obtained; it is not a verified manufacturer asset. Overall housing, lead envelope and pitch are reference facts; cavities only show mating locations. Internal contact geometry and retention are omitted. Used for enclosure clearances and nominal insertion depth only.

Sources: https://suddendocs.samtec.com/catalog_english/esq_th.pdf and https://suddendocs.samtec.com/prints/hle-1xx-02-xxx-dv-xx-xx-x-xx-mkt.pdf .

## Making and assembly

This reference is not a printed or manufactured part. Purchase the exact part in the BOM. Prototype insertion, soldering and retention need physical confirmation.
